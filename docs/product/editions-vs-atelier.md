# ORVAUXE Editions vs Atelier

Editions and Atelier are two valid ways to engage ORVAUXE. The choice is based on fit, not on a hierarchy of quality: Editions starts from a proven product system; Atelier creates a system for requirements that cannot responsibly fit one.

## Comparison

| Dimension                | Editions                                                                   | Atelier                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Best fit                 | A brand that already fits a named Edition’s visual and commerce logic      | A brand whose strategy or requirements call for a new direction                      |
| Art direction            | Fixed underlying Edition DNA                                               | Bespoke art direction                                                                |
| Information architecture | Predefined product architecture and template family                        | Custom information architecture and new template types                               |
| Customization            | Controlled brand adaptation inside approved settings and composition rules | Scope-defined custom design and interaction systems                                  |
| Commerce logic           | Native Shopify theme and standard product/cart foundations                 | Unusual product logic, custom integrations or other architecture as justified        |
| Runtime                  | Native Shopify Online Store theme                                          | Native Shopify, headless/Hydrogen or another approved architecture according to need |
| Delivery model           | Repeatable system with a target of 7–10 business days from Ready to Build  | Project plan based on discovery, dependencies and approved scope                     |
| Public starting price    | From **$2,490**                                                            | From **$6,000**                                                                      |
| Revisions                | 2 consolidated revision rounds in the baseline                             | Defined by the bespoke statement of work                                             |
| Outcome                  | A selected Edition, recognizably adapted to the client’s brand             | A new solution designed around the client’s specific strategy and constraints        |

Starting prices are positioning baselines, not automatic quotes. Final scope, dependencies and external costs must be approved before work begins.

## What stays within an Edition

An Edition remains the right product when the request:

- keeps the selected Edition’s underlying art direction;
- uses its existing template and section vocabulary;
- preserves its product UX, navigation model, responsive logic and core composition;
- can be delivered through approved theme settings, content, imagery and minor coherent tuning;
- uses native Shopify primitives and practical app-block insertion points;
- is bounded enough to estimate without open-ended design discovery.

## Add-on candidates

A request may be quoted as an Edition add-on when it stays inside the Edition architecture but adds a measurable amount of work. Candidates include:

- an additional content population pass;
- an additional consolidated revision round;
- straightforward localization or market work beyond the base initial setup;
- configuration of a known, compatible app through an approved app block;
- an additional page instance composed entirely from the approved section vocabulary;
- limited catalog migration or cleanup with a defined record count and source quality.

This list is not a promise of inclusion. Each item requires a dependency, price and schedule review; complex variants can still escalate to Atelier.

## Atelier triggers

The request moves to Atelier qualification when it requires one or more of the following:

- completely new or materially different art direction;
- major navigation or information-architecture redesign;
- a new custom template family;
- complex interactive storytelling or a novel interaction model;
- custom Shopify app or backend development;
- an account/customer portal beyond agreed native capabilities;
- advanced subscription, loyalty or B2B architecture;
- unusual product, cart or checkout behavior;
- headless delivery or Hydrogen;
- ERP, PIM, WMS, complex CRM or other heavy enterprise integration;
- open-ended strategy, migration or product-data remediation.

Atelier is not an “upgraded Edition.” It is a different engagement model for work whose value depends on creating new architecture or direction.

## Change-order procedure

1. Capture the requested outcome and why the current Edition boundary does not satisfy it.
2. Assess art direction, template architecture, commerce behavior, integration, content, access and schedule impact.
3. Classify the request as included, Edition add-on or Atelier qualification.
4. Document the changed deliverable, dependency, price and timeline before implementation.
5. Obtain consolidated approval. Pause the affected Edition work when missing input or an unresolved change makes continued work unsafe.

An approved add-on preserves the Edition identity. If the cumulative add-ons undermine that identity or delivery model, the engagement is re-qualified for Atelier rather than assembled into an unbounded Edition.
