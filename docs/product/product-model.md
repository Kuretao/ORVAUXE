# ORVAUXE Product Model

## Editions

Productized premium storefront systems, approximately **$2,490–$3,490** at initial positioning. An Edition is a curated launch foundation adapted to the client’s brand; it is never described as a cheap template.

Core promise: a stronger starting point, controlled scope and faster path to a considered launch.

## Atelier

Semi-custom and bespoke commerce strategy, design and development, approximately **$6,000–$15,000+**. Atelier is selected when the brand requires a unique content model, interaction language, complex migration or deeper design and technical work.

## Care

Post-launch support and continuous improvement, approximately **$199–$499+/month** in a future offer. Care is not a launch headline. It is introduced after project fit and service capacity are validated.

## Concept vs client work

Until real client projects exist, showcase entries are ORVAUXE Originals or Edition Concepts. Client terminology and performance results are reserved for work with verifiable evidence.
